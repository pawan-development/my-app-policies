Norvix SchoolOS Privacy Policy

App: Norvix SchoolOS (Android, version 1.0) · Effective date: October 29, 2026

Version 1.0 of Norvix SchoolOS is an offline demo. It shows fictional sample data that is packaged inside the app, and it does not send information to the developer or to any third party.

1. Who we are

Norvix SchoolOS is published under the name Norvix. This page refers to the publisher as “we”. For questions about this policy, see Contact.

2. Scope

This policy applies to Norvix SchoolOS for Android, version 1.0 (package com.norvix.schoolos). It describes how that release behaves. It does not cover any other app, website or future version that adds new features.

3. Information the app handles

Sample data bundled in the appNames, classes, attendance, results, fees, messages and announcements for a fictional school. These records are packaged inside the app and are not about real people. They are read on your device and are never uploaded.
Information you enterThe start screen shows a username and password form. In this version, nothing typed there is sent anywhere or saved (see Sign-in and backend). The demo screens are read-only, so you cannot add or change records.
Information stored on your deviceOne small setting: the identifier of the sample demo profile you last opened (for example, the sample teacher, student or parent profile). It does not identify you.
Information sent over a networkNone by the app’s own code in this release. The release build is configured without a server address, so it has nowhere to send data.
Third-party SDKsThe app includes no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or attribution SDKs. See Sharing and third parties.

4. Offline demo mode

From the start screen you can choose Explore Demo App, then pick the Teacher, Student or Parent role. You can then browse the app using the bundled sample data. This works without an account and without an internet connection.

Your interactions in the demo, such as opening screens, are not saved, other than the demo profile setting described below. Actions that would change data are not performed in the demo.

5. Sign-in and backend

Version 1.0 does not require a school backend or a live account. The start screen still displays a username and password form. In this version, submitting it only shows a message that school-account sign-in is not available yet. Nothing you type is transmitted or stored, and no account can be created.

A future version may add school-account sign-in. Before it is released, we will update this policy and the information we provide in Google Play.

6. Storage, retention and deletion

  • The demo profile identifier is kept on your device in Android’s encrypted storage (through the Expo SecureStore library). The app configures it to be left out of Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer.
  • It is removed when you choose Exit demo in the app, when you clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or when you uninstall the app.
  • We hold no server-side record about you, because this version has no accounts and sends us no data. There is nothing for us to delete on a server.

7. Sharing and third-party services

We do not receive information from the app, so we do not share any. The app is built with open-source libraries (such as React Native and Expo) that run on your device. Our review of the app’s dependencies found no library that sends data to a third party in this release. Google Play and your device manufacturer may collect their own information under their own privacy policies when you install or update the app.

8. Children and students

Norvix SchoolOS is school-management software for teachers, parents and students. The demo includes a sample Student portal so you can see what that view looks like. The app is intended for users aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. Because this version collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from students or children. All student records shown are fictional.

9. Security

Because this version sends no personal information, there is little to protect in transit. The one stored setting is kept in Android’s encrypted storage. No method of storage or electronic transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Android permissions

INTERNETStandard permission included by the app framework. This version makes no network requests of its own.
ACCESS_NETWORK_STATEAdded by framework libraries to check connectivity. No personal data is involved.
VIBRATEAdded by the app framework’s defaults. It gives no access to data.

The app does not request location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files, or any other permission that asks you at runtime.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and its effective date. We will do so before releasing any version that handles personal information differently from what is described here.
